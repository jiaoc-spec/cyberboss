# CyberBoss Growth Operating System

## Unified Objective

CyberBoss helps Jane become a high-performance person without turning her life
into a productivity dashboard.

High performance means:

- important long-term values remain visible during busy real life
- focus time is protected
- recovery is treated as infrastructure, not failure
- knowledge is converted into decisions and real outputs
- reminders are timely but limited
- daily evidence compounds into weekly, monthly, and long-range understanding

Every module must serve this objective. A module that creates more noise than
useful action should stay silent.

## Decision Stack

1. **Current reality**: explicit current state plus Day Operations Plan.
2. **Protection**: work, course, commute, focus, sleep, and recovery windows.
3. **Intervention budget**: the Proactive Intervention Coordinator selects at
   most a small number of unsolicited interventions per day.
4. **Priority**: explicit boundaries and Level A values before lower-priority
   growth prompts.
5. **Knowledge leverage**: recall old knowledge only when it helps a current
   task, decision, project, or output.
6. **Experience compounding**: convert repeated review lessons into reusable
   action guides, not raw note piles.
7. **Review**: turn evidence into patterns and one useful adjustment, not a
   backend report.

## Capability Map

### Context Engine v2 and Assistant Command Center

- Resolve replies to pending questions.
- Use the persisted Day Operations Plan as the canonical day type.
- Block home-only or sleep advice during work/course/commute.
- Keep high-context turns on the primary runtime.

### Recovery-Aware Planning and Executive Assistant Rhythm

- Day Operations Planner defines fixed blocks, recovery buffers, and useful
  priority windows.
- Executive Assistant Rhythm v2 turns that plan into a single leverage point:
  one first move, one protected identity lane, and no unsolicited Level B/C
  stacking unless a campaign or deadline makes it truly relevant.
- Day Strategy, Critical Habits, Priority Awareness, Missing Context, active
  recall, digestion, decision follow-up, and random companionship all share one
  proactive budget.
- Explicit reminders, Focus completion, safety boundaries, and review pipelines
  remain deterministic and do not consume the ordinary conversation budget.

### Just-in-Time Insight Recall

- Activates only for real work such as studying, writing, teaching preparation,
  research, planning, or an important decision.
- Searches concept cards, notes, weekly/monthly reviews, Pattern Ledger,
  Experience Ledger, Decision Journal, Research Ledger, and active Campaigns.
- Injects a few relevant items into the current reply; it does not send another
  notification.
- Academic evidence, reusable experience, personal patterns, and past decisions
  remain labeled as different evidence types.

### Experience Compound System

- Daily Review extracts only 0-3 reusable lessons, principles, or insights.
- Experience Ledger groups them by stable theme and controlled domain.
- Repeated themes become action-guide candidates after enough evidence.
- Weekly/Monthly Review may turn candidates into practical action guides.
- Monthly Review refreshes the Obsidian Experience Compound Dashboard.
- Normal chat does not announce ledger writes; the value appears later through
  review, dashboard, and just-in-time recall.

### Knowledge Portfolio and Source Reliability

- Concept cards carry `source`, `source_type`, and `evidence_status`.
- Unknown information stays `unknown` / `unverified`.
- Monthly audit checks atomicity proxies, tags, missing sources, broken links,
  orphan cards, duplicate titles, and active themes.
- The Long-Term Memory Dashboard is updated through one managed Obsidian block,
  so repeated updates do not duplicate the page.

### Output / Project Hub

The existing Campaign system is the project hub. It stores:

- kind and status
- one current next action
- linked Obsidian notes
- concrete outputs
- deadlines and temporary habit boosts

This avoids a second project database. Hausarbeit, Seminar, teaching material,
research proposals, applications, and paper drafts use Campaign plus Obsidian.

### Research and Academic Writing

- Monthly review may propose up to three research-question candidates when
  enough evidence exists.
- Candidates must name their source themes or patterns and remain hypotheses
  until Jane confirms one.
- Academic writing uses Campaign outputs, linked notes, source reliability, and
  just-in-time recall; it does not create another writing ledger.

## Conflict Rules

- Day Operations Plan overrides conflicting day-type guesses.
- Explicit current state overrides older assumptions about location/activity.
- Focus and fixed calendar blocks suppress ordinary proactive prompts.
- Priority Awareness may exceed the daily budget only near a genuine hard
  boundary, but never during a fixed work/course block.
- Recovery can downgrade a task to its minimum version or permit conscious
  rest; comfort must not erase chosen values.
- Level C, knowledge audits, research-question generation, and graph maintenance
  never compete with Level A on the same day.
- Personal observation never becomes academic evidence without a source.
- Experience Ledger stores reusable personal lessons; Pattern Ledger stores
  longitudinal patterns. Do not collapse them into one noisy ledger.
